Visualizing East Bay Punk

VEBP

A suite of interactive visualizations exploring the networks, geography, events, and cultural production of the Bay Area punk scene.

  • People

    Network graph of band members and their interconnections across the scene.

  • Places

    Interactive map of punk venues, cultural hotspots, and counter-infrastructural nodes.

  • Words

    Lyrics analysis of all Lookout Records releases, 1987–1994.

  • Shows

    Searchable database of 8,000+ punk performances across the Bay Area.

  • Gilman

    Performance history of the Gilman Street Project, 1986–1994.
