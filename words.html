    Overview

    This application visualizes lyrics from all Lookout Records releases from 1987 to 1994.

    The All Records tab provides a word cloud of the overall corpus; clicking on a word shows its associations in a network graph.

    The Individual Records tab lets you select a release to view its tracks, word cloud, and word association network.

    The Randomizer generates a mashup of lyric lines from across the dataset.

    Below each word cloud, the most frequent words are also listed as buttons, and each association network is summarised as a list, so everything can be explored with a keyboard or screen reader.

    The Dataset

    Lyrics were transcribed from liner notes or scraped from Genius.com, Plyrics, and Letras.com.
