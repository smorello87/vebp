Places

Interactive map of punk venues, cultural hotspots, and counter-infrastructural nodes.

How to use this visualization
  • Each marker is a place; its icon and the label in its popup give the kind of place (venue, punk house, label…).
  • Search by name or address, or switch categories on and off. The count below updates as you filter.
  • The list of places under the filters mirrors the map: choose a place to zoom to it and open its details.
  • On the map, drag or use the arrow keys to pan, and the + and − buttons or keys to zoom. Markers can be reached with Tab.
