How to use this visualization
  • Search for a band or member: matches and their direct connections stay highlighted, the view zooms to them, and the connections are listed as text below the search box.
  • Path finder: enter two names to highlight every shortest chain of connections between them.
  • Filter by connections fades nodes with fewer connections than the slider value.
  • Drag the background to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, or use the zoom buttons. Drag a node to move it.
